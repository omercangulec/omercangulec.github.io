Poptri Support

Poptri is a falling-block match-three puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Ömer Can Güleç, an independent developer.

Contact

For questions, bug reports or ideas, write to omercangulec.games@gmail.com. I usually reply within a few days.

If you are reporting a bug, it helps to include your phone or tablet model, its system version (for example iOS 26 or Android 16), the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot is very welcome.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag left and right to move the falling block, tap to change the order of its symbols, and touch and hold to make it fall faster. Line up three or more of the same symbol horizontally, vertically or diagonally to pop them. You can see the full guide any time in Settings › How to play.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app or change phones?

Yes. Poptri has no accounts, and your best score, coins, bombs and themes are saved only on your device. Deleting the app, or moving to a new phone, starts the game from the beginning. Your progress cannot be restored, because it is never sent anywhere.

A button says "No ad right now". What does that mean?

Bombs, extra coins and some other rewards come from short videos. Sometimes no video is available, for example when you are not connected to the internet. The game keeps working; try again a little later.

Why can't I watch more videos for coins today?

Coin videos are limited to 5 a day. The limit resets at midnight.

When does the daily challenge change?

A new challenge starts every day at midnight, using your phone's time. Everyone gets the same challenge on the same day. You have one free try each day; if you don't make it, you can get up to two more by watching a short video. Once you complete it, see you tomorrow.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

Open Settings › Privacy settings in the game. The Privacy Policy explains what data is used for ads and how to change your choices.
