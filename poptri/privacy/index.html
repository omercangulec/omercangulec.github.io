Poptri Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Poptri is a puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Ömer Can Güleç, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play Poptri, by whom, and the choices you have.

In short

  • There are no accounts. I don't ask for your name, email or anything else about you.
  • Your game progress stays on your phone. It is never sent to me.
  • Poptri is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. Ads chosen for you are only shown if you allow them.

Information stored on your device

Poptri saves your best score, coins, bombs, the themes you own and picked, your daily reward progress and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language). This is stored only on your phone, and I cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Poptri does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not collect your contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Poptri shows banner ads, full-screen ads between games, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for bombs, coins, or to continue a game). The ads are provided by Google AdMob, operated by Google. To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), when available,
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device information such as model, operating system, language and screen size,
  • how you interact with ads (for example, which ads were shown or tapped).

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy, as an independent controller. I do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. More information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices about ads

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: when you first open Poptri, a consent form (Google's certified consent tool) asks whether you agree to personalized ads and related data use. You can change your choice at any time in Poptri under Settings › Privacy settings. Without consent, Google shows non-personalized ads, which may still use limited data for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • iPhone: Poptri asks for permission to track before using your advertising identifier. If you don't allow it, the identifier is not shared. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (the exact path may differ by phone).
  • You can manage ad personalization across Google services at My Ad Center.

In-app purchases

If Poptri offers a paid purchase (for example, removing ads), payment is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. I never receive your payment details.

Children

Poptri is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Poptri does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been processed through Poptri, please contact me and I will help.

Legal basis and your rights

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) or Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) apply, personalized advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Non-personalized advertising relies on the legitimate interest of keeping the game free.

You have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Since I don't hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google through the links above, but you can always contact me and I will help.

Google may process data on servers outside your country, with the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
